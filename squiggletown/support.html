Squiggle Town

Support

Squiggle Town is an independently developed iPhone and iPad handwriting practice app. This page provides help for parents, carers and other grown-ups using the app with a child.

Getting help

For a problem with Squiggle Town, contact the developer through the Crunchy Codes GitHub profile. Please include the device model, iOS or iPadOS version, Squiggle Town version, what you were doing, and what happened. Do not include a child's full name, handwriting data or other private information in a public message.

Contact the developer on GitHub

Before reporting a problem

Check that the device is running a supported version of iOS or iPadOS, close and reopen Squiggle Town, and confirm that enough free device storage is available. If the problem affects one particular letter or activity, include that detail in the report.

Accounts and internet access

Squiggle Town is designed to work locally on the device and does not require a child account or cloud sign-in for its core practice flow.

Handwriting feedback

Squiggle Town provides practice-oriented, neutral feedback. It is not a diagnostic, therapeutic or clinical handwriting assessment. Questions about a child's handwriting development should be discussed with an appropriate teacher or qualified professional.

Privacy

See the Squiggle Town privacy policy for information about learner profiles, practice records and data export.
